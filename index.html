Why TechMountain

Built for Malawi, not adapted for it.

Every choice we make — from page weight to payment methods — assumes you are learning on a modest phone with a limited data bundle.

📶

Designed for 2G connections

Every course loads in under 2 seconds on a slow connection. Images are compressed, fonts are local, and lessons work offline after the first visit.

💻

Learn by doing, not watching

Every lesson has a live code sandbox. Real Python, real JavaScript, real Node.js, running in your browser. No installs, no setup.

🆓

Free, forever

No fees, no subscriptions, no fine print. Every course, every lesson, free for everyone — because education should not be a luxury.

👥

Community-first

WhatsApp and Telegram study groups. Instructors who reply. Alumni who help. You are never learning alone, no matter where you are.
